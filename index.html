幻影應變Phantom Strain

大雪過後，學校的滑雪行程被迫暫停：一根鋼製吊桿比設計圖長了 4%，而且沒有彈回原狀。After the blizzard, the school ski trip stops: one steel hanger rod is 4 % longer than its drawing — and it won't spring back.

現場留下一張卡片：「我將偷走你們鋼鐵的彈性。── 幻影應變」A card was left behind: "I shall steal the spring of your steel. — Phantom Strain"

是怪盜幹的？還是應力–應變曲線在說故事？跟著六條線索，照曲線的順序調查！A phantom thief — or a story told by the stress–strain curve? Follow six clues, in the order of the curve!

線索一：雪到底「多用力」拉這根鋼棒？力要除以原始面積，才是應力 σ = F/A₀。Clue 1: How hard did the snow pull? Divide force by the original area: σ = F/A₀.

打破題目箱！答對就能收集寶石、充能大招。小心拉力章魚和剪刀怪！Smash question boxes! Correct answers give gems and charge your super. Watch out for tentacles and blades!

線索二：幻影說要偷走「彈性」。彈性變形是可逆的，卸載就恢復。Clue 2: The phantom wants to steal "elasticity". Elastic deformation is reversible.

看看你的血條：它就是應力–應變曲線！在彈性區休息一下，變形會完全恢復。Look at your health bar — it IS a stress–strain curve! Rest in the elastic region and you fully recover.

線索三：鋼棒回不去了。超過降伏強度 σy 後，原子面滑移，變形變成永久。Clue 3: The rod never came back. Beyond σy, atomic planes slip and deformation becomes permanent.

超過抗拉強度 TS 後會頸縮──現場那根鋼棒中間就有「腰身」！Past TS comes necking — the rod at the scene had a "waist"!

線索四：鋼棒是慢慢伸長，還是突然斷？延性與韌性決定一切。Clue 4: Would it stretch slowly or snap? Ductility and toughness decide.

韌性 = 曲線下的總面積。強度最高的英雄，不一定最耐打喔！Toughness = total area under the curve. The strongest hero isn't always the toughest!

線索五：工程曲線在 TS 之後下降，金屬真的變弱了嗎？Clue 5: The engineering curve drops after TS. Did the metal really get weaker?

用真實面積算真應力 σT = σ(1+ε)，它根本沒變弱。銅英雄越打越強就是應變硬化！Use the real area: σT = σ(1+ε). It never weakened. Copper getting stronger is strain hardening!

線索六：換上的鋼棒真的「等同」嗎？一個壓痕就能讀出強度：TS ≈ 3.45 × HB。Clue 6: Was the replacement rod really "equivalent"? One dent tells: TS ≈ 3.45 × HB.

呵呵……想知道真相，先打倒我的巨型橫梁機吧！Heh… want the truth? Defeat my Great Crosshead first!

真相只有一個：暴風雪中吊桿承受約 265 MPa，但換裝鋼棒的 σy 只有約 200 MPa。The truth: in the storm the rod carried ~265 MPa, but the replacement's σy was only ~200 MPa.

應力超過降伏強度 → 永久塑性應變 4% → 頸縮。不是怪盜，是選材錯誤！Stress exceeded yield → 4 % permanent plastic strain → necking. Not a thief — a materials mistake!

今晚我什麼也不偷。偵探們，記得選 σy ≥ 1.5 × 265 ≈ 398 MPa、又有延性的鋼！I steal nothing tonight. Detectives — choose a ductile steel with σy ≥ 1.5 × 265 ≈ 398 MPa!
